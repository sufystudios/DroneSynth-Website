DroneSynth

A hands-on, analog-style synthesizer built for ambient textures, evolving pads, and hypnotic drones.

iPhone · iPad · Mac · Android — and plugins for Logic Pro, GarageBand and Windows DAWs

What's inside

  • Three oscillators — sine, triangle, square, saw or pink noise, each with its own volume, octave, semitone, fine tune and vibrato.
  • Moog Ladder filter with a full ADSR envelope and glide. Stereo Filters, a one-time purchase, splits the filter across left and right for wider tones.
  • Three LFOs for the filter and the oscillators' pitch, including a wandering Random shape.
  • Delay and reverb — a vintage tape delay with tap tempo and ping-pong, washed through Room, Hall, Plate or Cathedral.
  • Drone mode holds a note without keeping a key down, for ambient beds and long textures.
  • 105 built-in sounds in eight categories, plus your own presets, as many as you like.
  • ARP/SEQ — an arpeggiator, and a step sequencer you record by playing.
  • Looper and recorder — layer a loop, record a take and share it.
  • Master tuning, including 432 Hz and other pitches.
  • MOTION — record your knob moves as a loop, and play another device from your phone. More about MOTION →
