Features

  • 3 independent oscillators — sine, triangle, square, saw and pink noise — each with octave, semitone, fine tune and vibrato
  • Moog Ladder filter, with optional true stereo filtering (Stereo Filters, a one-time purchase)
  • Full ADSR envelope, glide, and three LFOs for the filter and the oscillators' pitch, including a wandering Random shape
  • Vintage tape delay with tap tempo, ping-pong and tape character, and reverb across Room, Hall, Plate and Cathedral
  • ARP/SEQ: an arpeggiator and a step sequencer you record by playing
  • Looper and recorder: layer a loop, record a take and share it
  • Drone mode, master tuning (including 432 Hz and other pitches), and unlimited saved presets
  • MOTION — record your knob moves and notes as a loop, and use your phone or iPad as a wireless controller for the DroneSynth plugin (a one-time purchase)

Plugins for your DAW

The same synth runs as a plugin in your music software, with the whole synth on one hardware-style panel.

iPad and Mac: Audio Unit (AUv3)

Install DroneSynth from the App Store and open it once. It then appears as an instrument in Logic Pro, GarageBand and any AUv3 host: add it to a track, play it from your MIDI keyboard, and automate every knob.

Windows: VST3, CLAP and a standalone app (free preview)

Works in Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, REAPER, Bitwig and any other VST3 or CLAP host. Download it and follow the guide below.

DroneSynth for Windows

A free preview of DroneSynth for 64-bit Windows: a VST3 and CLAP plugin and a standalone app, with the same hardware panel as the iPad and Mac plugin and an ARP/SEQ that follows your DAW's tempo.

Download for Windows (.zip) Version 0.3.1 · 3.5 MB · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

1. Install

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it (right-click › Extract All…).
  2. Copy the DroneSynth.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  3. For a CLAP host (REAPER, Bitwig, FL Studio), also copy DroneSynth.clap to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\ (create the folder if it isn't there).
  4. Windows asks for permission to copy into Program Files: click Continue.

2. Add it to your DAW

  1. Rescan your plugins. For example: Ableton Live › Options › Preferences › Plug-Ins › Rescan; FL Studio › Options › Manage plugins › Find more plugins; REAPER › Options › Preferences › Plug-ins › VST › Re-scan.
  2. Find Drone Synth by Sufy Studios among your instruments and add it to a MIDI or instrument track.
  3. Arm or select the track and play your MIDI keyboard. The window opens with the full panel.

Or play it on its own

Double-click DroneSynth.exe to play without a DAW. It plays through your computer's speakers or headphones, has a keyboard under the panel (click the keys, or use ‹ and › to change octave), and listens to any MIDI keyboard you plug in. It isn't code-signed yet, so the first time Windows may say it protected your PC: click More info › Run anyway.

Using the panel

  • Knobs: drag up or down. Hold Shift for fine adjustment, scroll over a knob to nudge it, and double-click to reset it.
  • OSC 1 / 2 / 3, the three LFOs and ECHO / SPACE share their columns: click the small tabs to switch.
  • ARP / SEQ: set MODE to ARP, press PLAY and hold some keys. With FOLLOW HOST lit, it locks to your DAW's tempo while the transport plays. In SEQ mode, REC records a pattern as you play; patterns are saved with your project.
  • TUNE sets the whole synth's pitch: drag the small knob, or use − and + to move a cent at a time.
  • STOP DRONE silences anything left sounding.

3. Play it from your phone (optional)

With MOTION in the DroneSynth app on Android, iPhone or iPad, every knob you turn and key you play on the phone plays the plugin on your PC over Wi-Fi, and your DAW can record it as automation.

  1. Install the free rtpMIDI driver on your PC and open it.
  2. Under My Sessions, click + to add a session, and tick Enabled. Set Who may connect to me to Anyone. If Windows Firewall asks, allow rtpMIDI on private networks.
  3. In your DAW's MIDI settings, turn on the rtpMIDI session as an input (it's listed under its session name, such as "Session 1"), then choose it as the MIDI input of the Drone Synth track.
  4. On your phone, on the same Wi-Fi: open DroneSynth › MOTION › Controller, tap Controller to start sending, then tap your PC under NEARBY. It shows CONNECTED, and reconnects by itself next time.
  5. Tap Resend on the phone to send your phone's whole sound to the plugin. The plugin's MOTION light flickers while the phone plays.

Stereo Filters are a purchase in the phone app. Once you own them, switch STEREO on in the app while it's connected and the plugin turns them on too; your project remembers it.

Troubleshooting

The plugin doesn't appear in my DAW

Check the files are in the folders above (the VST3 is a folder called DroneSynth.vst3), then rescan. Some DAWs only list new plugins after a restart. It's 64-bit only, so it won't load in a 32-bit DAW.

The standalone app makes no sound

It plays through Windows' default output: check the speaker icon in the taskbar and choose your speakers or headphones there, then start the app again. Version 0.3 and earlier needed JACK to play; download 0.3.1.

My PC isn't listed on the phone

Both must be on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network). In rtpMIDI, the session must be ticked Enabled with "Who may connect to me" set to Anyone, and Windows Firewall must allow rtpMIDI. Restarting rtpMIDI often helps.

A note keeps sounding

Click STOP DRONE at the top of the panel. On the phone, turning Drone off or stopping the Controller also silences the plugin.

How it works

DroneSynth is built around three oscillators feeding a shared filter and envelope, with LFOs and a delay and reverb for movement and space.

  • OSC — pick a waveform (sine, triangle, square, saw, or pink noise) per oscillator and tune its volume, octave, semitone, and fine pitch.
  • ADSR — shape how each note attacks, decays, sustains, and releases.
  • LFO — low-frequency oscillators modulate filter cutoff and pitch for evolving, animated textures.
  • Delay & Reverb — tap out a tempo on the delay, bounce it ping-pong across the stereo field, then wash it through Room, Hall, Plate, or Cathedral reverb.
  • Drone — toggle Drone mode to sustain a note indefinitely without holding down keys, ideal for ambient beds and long-form textures.
  • ARP / SEQ — arpeggiate the keys you hold, or record a step pattern and transpose it as you play.
  • Presets — save and recall as many patches as you like from the Presets panel.
  • Stereo Filters — an optional one-time purchase that splits the Moog Ladder filter across the left and right channels for wider, more spacious tones.

MOTION & wireless controller

MOTION records the way you play — knob moves and notes — as a loop, and turns your iPhone, iPad or Android phone into a wireless controller for the DroneSynth plugin: in Logic Pro, GarageBand or any AUv3 host on a Mac or iPad, and in any VST3 or CLAP host on Windows. It's a one-time purchase in the app, unlocked from the MOTION tab. The plugin follows a controller for free.

Record a motion

  1. Open the MOTION tab — the last tab on the right-hand side — and choose Record.
  2. Choose the CLOCK: BARS loops in time with the tempo (pick 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars), SECS loops for as long as you record.
  3. Tap REC, then turn knobs and play notes. For a drone, turn Drone on and hold a note first — it's held right through the loop.
  4. Tap REC again to finish (in BARS it stops by itself at the end of the loop). It starts playing straight away.
  5. Each knob you moved gets its own lane: tap its light to mute it. Grab a knob while it plays to take over; it rejoins the motion when the loop comes round.
  6. Save or update your preset to keep the motion with it — each preset has its own. Stop the motion before saving, so the preset keeps your knobs where you set them.

Control the plugin on a Mac or iPad

  1. Put your phone or iPad and your Mac (or the iPad running your host) on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On a Mac, once: open Audio MIDI Setup › Window › Show MIDI Studio, click the globe (MIDI Network) icon, tick a session under My Sessions and set "Who may connect to me" to Anyone.
  3. In DroneSynth on your phone or iPad, open MOTION › Controller and tap Controller. Allow access to your local network if asked.
  4. Under NEARBY, tap your Mac or iPad. It shows CONNECTED, and reconnects by itself next time.
  5. In your host, add the DroneSynth plugin to a track and select that track. Every knob, preset and note you play now plays the plugin — and a motion playing on your phone does too.
  6. To record it as automation, set the track's automation to Latch or Touch (in Logic Pro) and press play.

Control the plugin on Windows

Windows needs the free rtpMIDI driver to receive network MIDI: see step 3 of the Windows guide.

Tips

  • Turn off Sound on this device too in MOTION to hear only the plugin.
  • Open the plugin before you start the Controller, or tap Resend afterwards, so the plugin gets your whole sound.
  • The host plays the selected (or record-enabled) track. Record-enable several DroneSynth tracks to play them all at once.
  • Tapping Sending to stop the controller silences the plugin, even if it's droning.
  • Keep the DroneSynth Mac app closed while you play the plugin on the same Mac, or it will play along too.
  • No computer on the list? Check both are on the same Wi-Fi, and that the session there lets anyone connect.

Questions

Is the Windows plugin free?

Yes. MOTION and Stereo Filters are optional purchases in the phone and iPad apps; the plugin follows them for free.

Do I need the phone app to use the Windows plugin?

No. It's a complete synth on its own: play it from your MIDI keyboard or its own keyboard. The app adds wireless control and the stereo filters.

Is there a VST3 for Mac?

On a Mac, use the Audio Unit that comes with the App Store app, in Logic Pro, GarageBand or any AU host. A Mac VST3 and CLAP for other DAWs are on the way.

Which phones can be a controller?

iPhone and iPad with DroneSynth, with MOTION unlocked. On Android, MOTION arrives in DroneSynth 3.2, coming soon to Google Play.
