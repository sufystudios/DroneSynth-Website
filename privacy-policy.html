Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Sufy Studios ("we," "us," or "our") built DroneSynth as a self-contained music synthesizer: an app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, an Audio Unit plugin, and a plugin and standalone app for Windows. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use any of them.

We do not collect any personal data

DroneSynth does not have a server, does not use analytics, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not track you or your device across apps or websites. Nothing you do in DroneSynth is sent to us.

What's stored, and where

Your presets, recorded motions, loops, recordings and preferences are saved only on your device (on Apple devices in the app's own storage, such as UserDefaults; on Android in the app's private storage). In a plugin, your sound is saved inside your DAW project. None of it is sent to us or any third party, and the app's copy is deleted if you delete the app.

Things you choose to send

  • Sharing: when you share a recording or a preset, it goes only where you send it, using your device's own share sheet.
  • MOTION and network MIDI: when you connect to another device on your Wi-Fi or over a cable (a Mac, iPad, PC or phone), DroneSynth sends it your knob moves and notes as MIDI, directly and only to the devices you connect. While it's open, the Android app shows this device's name to other devices on the same Wi-Fi (as Apple's network MIDI does) so they can connect to it. Nothing passes through us.

In-App Purchases

DroneSynth offers optional one-time purchases (such as Stereo Filters and MOTION), processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies — we never see or receive your payment details. The app only learns whether a purchase was completed, to unlock the feature on your device.

Ratings

Now and then the app may ask you to rate it, using Apple's or Google's own rating prompt. Your rating and review go to the App Store or Google Play under their policies; DroneSynth doesn't see them.

Children's Privacy

DroneSynth does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Third-Party Services

DroneSynth uses open-source audio libraries (such as AudioKit, Soundpipe and Oboe) that run entirely on your device to generate and process sound. None of them transmit data off your device. The Windows plugin and standalone app make no network connections.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same URL.
